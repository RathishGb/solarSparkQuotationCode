## Net Metering Solar Credit Rates

| Category | Credit Rate |
|----------|------------|
| Domestic | Rs 8.50/unit |
| Commercial | Rs 9.50/unit |
| Agricultural | Rs 5.50/unit |

### How Net Metering Works

**Your Bill = (Consumption - Solar Generation) × Slab Rate + Fixed Charge**

**Example: 220 units, 5kW solar**
- Solar generation: 675 units/month (5kW × 4.5 hrs × 30 days)
- Self-consumption: 540 units (80%)
- Export to grid: 135 units (20%)

**Calculation:**
```
Meter reading (consumption): 220 units
Less: Solar generation (self-use): -540 units
Net consumption: 0 (exceeded!)
Export credit: 135 × Rs 8.50 = Rs 1,147.50
Fixed charge: Rs 30
Net Bill: Rs 30 - Rs 1,147.50 = -Rs 1,117.50 (CREDIT!)
```

## Key Factors for Your Solar Recommendation

### 1. **Consumption Pattern**
- More consistent = Better solar ROI
- Variation >50% = Consider battery storage

### 2. **Current Slab Position**
- Slab 4 (301+) = Highest solar ROI
- Slab 1 (<100) = Lower ROI but still profitable

### 3. **Connection Type**
- Domestic: Best incentives & net metering
- Commercial: Higher rates but larger systems
- Agricultural: Heavily subsidized, best ROI %

### 4. **Roof Space**
- 5-6 sqm per kW needed
- South-facing exposure preferred

## Common Myths vs Reality

| Myth | Reality |
|------|---------|
| "Solar saves the whole bill" | No, fixed charge remains (~2-3% of bill) |
| "All consumption goes to solar" | 80% self-use only, export can't all be credited |
| "Solar pays for itself in 5 years" | Realistic payback: 12-15 years for 5kW |
| "Works the same everywhere" | TN has 4.5 peak sun hrs - varies by location |
